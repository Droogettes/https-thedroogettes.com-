About

Philadelphia's The Droogettes have been making noise since 2013. Equal parts street punk grit and pop hook glory, their "Sugar Oi!" sound sits somewhere between Cock Sparrer's working-class anthems and Blondie's downtown cool - melodic, hook-driven, and built for singing along. After a six-year hiatus, the all-female five-piece is back, louder and sharper than ever.
